Thank you for contributing to Andropia.

This agreement clarifies the intellectual property license granted with
contributions. It protects you, the project, and everyone who relies on it. It
is adapted from the Apache Software Foundation Individual CLA, which has been in
wide use for two decades.

**You keep your copyright.** This is not a copyright assignment. You retain full
ownership of everything you write and remain free to use it however you like,
including in other projects.

## Why this exists

Andropia is released under Apache-2.0 and intended to stay open. The agreement
exists so that the project can, if it ever becomes necessary, relicense or
offer commercial terms without needing to locate and obtain permission from
every past contributor individually — a situation that has stranded other
projects permanently.

If the project is ever relicensed, it will not be to terms more restrictive than
Apache-2.0 for existing users of existing releases.

## Assets are different

This agreement covers **code**. Contributions of 3D models, animations,
textures, audio, or fonts additionally require that you state the asset's
license and provenance, and that its terms permit redistribution, modification,
and commercial use by downstream users.

Assets whose license prohibits commercial use — including CC-BY-NC and
CC-BY-NC-SA — **cannot be accepted**, because they would restrict every
downstream user of the project. See `ASSET-LICENSES.md` for the standard applied
to bundled assets.

## How to sign

Signing is automated. Open a pull request and a bot will comment with a link;
signing takes a few seconds and is recorded once, covering all future
contributions.
